Slide the tiles into order and win XP – one reward a day!

How to Play

Tap a tile next to the empty space to slide it across (or use the arrow keys). Keep sliding until the numbers run 1 to 8 in order.

Objective

Arrange tiles 1–8 in order, with the empty space in the bottom-right corner.

Time & Attempts

90 seconds per attempt. You get 2 reward attempts per day.

Reward

Solve it to win XP: Gold +100, Silver +60, Bronze +30.

Score

Starts at 1,000 and drops with every move and second (minimum 100). Gold needs 750+, Silver 500+.

Eligibility

Open to every player at any level. Only a solved puzzle earns XP, and practice rounds earn none.

How Often

One reward per day, resetting at midnight your time. Replay for practice any time.
